# 📊 Olist E-Commerce Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI E-Commerce Dashboard** built to analyze sales performance, product revenue, regional performance, delivery efficiency, and customer-related metrics for the **Olist E-Commerce dataset**.

The dashboard provides a business-focused view of overall performance and helps identify revenue trends, high-performing product categories, regional revenue concentration, and delivery patterns across Brazilian states.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall e-commerce sales and revenue performance.
* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Analyze revenue distribution across Brazilian states.
* Track monthly and yearly revenue trends.
* Analyze product sales, orders, pricing, and freight values.
* Evaluate delivery performance across different regions.
* Identify states with higher average delivery times.
* Understand key business insights through interactive Power BI visualizations.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

* The business generated approximately **$16.01M in total revenue** from **96K orders** and **99K customers**.
* Product revenue accounts for approximately **$13.6M**.
* **Beleza Saúde** is the highest-revenue category shown in the dashboard, followed by **Relógios Presentes** and **Cama Mesa Banho**.
* Revenue is highly concentrated in major Brazilian states, particularly **São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais**.
* São Paulo contributes approximately **$5.8M** in revenue.
* The overall average delivery time is **12.5 days**.
* Approximately **7K orders** are identified as late.
* Roraima, Amapá, and Amazonas show some of the highest average delivery times in the dashboard.
* The dashboard indicates that regional delivery performance is an important area for further operational analysis.
